Claude x Affiliate Marketing

Use Claude as your research and content partner.

Claude can help you move from a rough product idea to useful, honest affiliate content. Keep your experience at the center, then use AI to sharpen the work around it.

01
Find a real buyer problem

Ask Claude to map audience questions, objections, and search intent for one niche. Verify claims against reliable sources.

02
Build a content angle

Give Claude your actual test results and ask for hooks, comparisons, short scripts, and a clear disclosure. Never invent a personal result.

03
Turn attention into trust

Use practical guides, transparent pros and cons, and a relevant marketplace link. Recommend only products that genuinely fit the reader.

04
Measure and improve

Ask Claude to compare click-through and conversion data, then improve one headline or call to action at a time. Follow every network's rules.

UGC Ads Guide

Make UGC ads that feel like recommendations.

User-generated content works when it shows a believable problem, a real product moment, and a clear next step. Keep the creator's voice natural and disclose the partnership.

01
Open with the problem

Start with a specific frustration your audience recognizes in the first few seconds.

02
Show the product in use

Use a close-up demo, an honest first impression, and one practical result instead of a polished sales pitch.

03
Share proof and limits

Explain who the product is for, what changed, and what it cannot do. Specificity creates trust.

04
Close with one action

Invite viewers to learn more or shop through the relevant link. Add an advertising disclosure where required.
